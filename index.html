Questions

Is it really free? +

Yes. The app is open source under the MIT license and has no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. If you'd like to leave a tip, you're welcome to.

How do I install it without the App Store? +

The App Store is the easiest way because you get updates automatically. If you prefer to sideload, download the .ipa from GitHub Releases and install it with AltStore or Sideloadly.

What comic formats does it read? +

It reads CBZ archives with JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, BMP or TIFF pages inside. CBR and PDF files aren't supported.

Does it work on iPad? +

Yes. You can read on iPhone and iPad in portrait or landscape, and on a bigger screen you see two pages side by side. You need iOS or iPadOS 26.

Is there a Mac version? +

Yes. One App Store download covers iPhone, iPad and Mac (macOS 14 or later), and the Mac app is also available through Homebrew and on GitHub. You can move your library between devices as one export file, including your progress, favorites and bookmarks.

Do you collect any data? +

No. You don't need an account, there are no analytics, and everything stays on your device.
